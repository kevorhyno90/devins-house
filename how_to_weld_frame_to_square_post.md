# How to Weld the Angle-Line Rectangle onto the Vertical Square Pipe Post

### The Problem:
* Your **vertical square post** is in the center (for example, 4"×4" or 5"×5").
* Your **angle-line rectangle frame** is on the outside border (30" wide × 10" deep).
* Because the rectangle frame is hollow in the middle, the angle lines are 10 inches apart and do not touch the small square post in the center.

Here are the **two easiest ways** to connect them:

---

## Method 1: The "Center Bridge" Angle Bar (Easiest & Strongest)

Weld **ONE extra piece of your 2" × 2" angle line** running front-to-back right down the middle of the rectangular frame:

```
+-----------------------------------------------------------+
|                    REAR ANGLE LINE                        |
|                                                           |
|                        [  CENTER  ]                       |
|                        [  BRIDGE  ]                       |
|                        [  ANGLE   ]                       |
|                        [   BAR    ]                       |
|                                                           |
|                    FRONT ANGLE LINE                       |
+-----------------------------------------------------------+
                                ||
                    [ VERTICAL SQUARE PIPE ]
                    [      POST UNDER      ]
```

### How to do it:
1. Make your rectangular angle-line frame (front, back, left, right).
2. Cut one 10-inch piece of angle line.
3. Weld it inside the frame, running from the front angle line to the back angle line, right down the center.
4. Set the frame on top of your vertical square post.
5. The center piece sits **directly on the top of the square pipe post**.
6. Weld the top of the square pipe to this center angle bar.
* **Why this is great**: It gives you a rock-solid welded connection, and it supports the middle of the timber so the wood never flexes.

---

## Method 2: Two Front-to-Back Angle Runners (Cradle on the Post)

Instead of putting a bar inside the rectangle, weld two angle-line "runner arms" directly to the sides of the vertical square post first:

```
              +-------------------------------+
              |   REAR ANGLE LINE OF FRAME    |
              +-------------------------------+
                       |             |
     Left Runner Angle |  [SQUARE ]  | Right Runner Angle
     (Welded to Post)  |  [ POST  ]  | (Welded to Post)
                       |  [       ]  |
              +-------------------------------+
              |   FRONT ANGLE LINE OF FRAME   |
              +-------------------------------+
```

### How to do it:
1. Cut two pieces of angle line to the depth of the step (10 inches / 250 mm).
2. Weld one piece to the **left side** of your vertical square post, and one to the **right side** of the square post, running horizontally front-to-back.
3. These two pieces create a 10-inch wide flat shelf on top of your post.
4. Drop your rectangular frame onto these two arms.
5. Weld the front and back of your rectangular frame directly to these two arms.

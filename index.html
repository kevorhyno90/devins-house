6H. How to Build the Timber & Metal Mezzanine Slab (Step-by-Step Construction Guide)

Isolated technical guide of the mezzanine floor slab assembly only. Shows exact layer-by-layer anatomy: pillar mounting baseplates, structural steel I-beams, 50×150mm timber joists, acoustic sound dampening, 18mm tongue-and-groove structural plywood subfloor, and oak floorboards.

SLAB ANATOMY & ASSEMBLY
🏗️ 3D Exploded Layer Diagram: Slab Assembly Only
3D Exploded Construction Layers
📐 2D Technical Cross-Section Detail: Layer Stack
Step # Phase Name Materials & Fasteners How to Build (Contractor Instructions) Pro-Tip to Eliminate Squeaks & Noise
STEP 1 Pillar Anchor Baseplates Four 200×200×10mm steel baseplates + 4 × M16 galvanized anchor bolts per pillar Embed or drill chemically anchored M16 threaded rods 200mm deep into the top of each of the 4 concrete/masonry pillars. Level the baseplates with high-strength non-shrink grout. Ensure baseplates are 100% laser-level before tightening nuts to avoid twisting steel beams.
STEP 2 Hoist Primary Steel I-Beams 150×75mm Universal Beams (approx. 14 kg/m; total weight ~112 kg per 4m beam) Lift the two main steel beams onto the pillar baseplates. Bolt or stitch-weld beam bottom flanges directly to the anchor plates. Prime with red oxide anti-rust coating. Two workers can easily lift these lightweight steel beams manually without needing a crane or hoist truck.
STEP 3 Install Timber Joists & Hangers 50×150mm (2"×6") kiln-dried C24 treated timber joists @ 400mm (16") spacing + galvanized steel joist hangers Seat timber joists across the beams spaced exactly 400mm on-center. Secure each joist with heavy-duty metal joist hangers using 38mm galvanized twist nails. Install solid timber cross-blocking (noggins) every 1.5m along the joist span to lock joists vertically and stop bounce.
STEP 4 Acoustic Insulation Pack 75mm high-density Rockwool / mineral wool insulation batts (density 45 kg/m³) Press insulation batts snugly between the timber joists. Ensure zero air gaps between bays. Run all electrical conduit pipes through pre-drilled holes in the center third of joists. Mineral wool absorbs sound reverberation and prevents footstep noise from reaching the kids room below (STC 52).
STEP 5 Glue & Screw Plywood Subfloor 18mm exterior-grade tongue-and-groove structural plywood / OSB-3 + polyurethane subfloor adhesive + 50mm Torx deck screws Lay sheets perpendicular to joists with tongue-and-groove joints tightly interlocked. Apply continuous beads of adhesive along top of joists. Fasten screws every 150mm at edges and 300mm in field. Never nail the subfloor. Screwing and gluing with polyurethane adhesive prevents 100% of timber floor squeaks.
STEP 6 Underlay & Finished Floor 3mm acoustic rubber/EVA foam underlayment + 14mm click-lock oak hardwood or SPC luxury vinyl planks Roll out acoustic underlay with taped seams. Install tongue-and-groove hardwood planks leaving an 8mm expansion gap along perimeter walls hidden by baseboards. The 3mm rubber underlay decouples the hardwood planks from the subfloor, absorbing impact vibrations from walking.

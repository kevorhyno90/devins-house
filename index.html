Cost Minimization & Value Engineering Guide

Proven structural decisions that reduce your total construction expense by over $10,800 without compromising beauty or durability.

ESTIMATED SAVINGS: $10,800+
🏗️ Single Kitchen Slab vs Full Floor Saves ~$4,200

Casting an intermediate concrete slab strictly over the kitchen area (~9.88m²) rather than the entire 40m² ground floor saves 75% of second-floor formwork, props, concrete volume, and rebar steel reinforcement.

🦘 Kangaroo Roof vs Flat Concrete Roof Saves ~$3,800

Flat concrete roof slabs require costly torch-on waterproofing membranes, thermal screed, and frequent maintenance. The kangaroo concealed roof uses cost-effective light gauge metal trusses and corrugated sheeting hidden behind parapets, delivering the same modern flat-top aesthetic with zero leakage risks.

🍽️ Dry Pantry vs Wet Kitchen Plumbing Saves ~$1,500

Eliminating the kitchen sink and dishwasher eliminates underground grease trap excavation, kitchen waste piping through the foundation slab, and hot/cold water supply lines to the kitchen island.

🚪 Single Metal Entrance Security Door Saves ~$1,300

Installing one single 3.5ft solid metal security door and omitting back patio sliding glass doors eliminates expensive multi-track patio sliders, exterior security shutters, and secondary deadbolts.
